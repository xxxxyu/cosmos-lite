# Code Of Conduct

Cosmos Lite welcomes technical discussion and contributions from people of all
backgrounds. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work rather
than the person, assume good faith, and make room for differing levels of
experience.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable. This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project spaces.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove inappropriate content and may restrict
participation when necessary. Moderation decisions are made in the interest of
keeping the project technically useful and safe to participate in.

Report conduct concerns privately to `xiangyu.sdlc@foxmail.com`. Include links
or screenshots and enough context to evaluate the report. Reports are handled
confidentially and on a best-effort basis.
